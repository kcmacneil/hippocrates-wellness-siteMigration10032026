Everything at Hippocrates flows from The Life Transformation Program.

People arrive here at a turning point. Often when they have tried everything. They’re ready for something real. Each journey meets that moment with the same seventy-year foundation: Right Food, Right Mind, Right Practice, Right People, Right Place.

Your body already knows how to live. The work is to restore the conditions — the food, the rhythm, the rest, the people — and let seventy years of practice carry the rest.

Integrative health education and living food come together to help the body return to the conditions in which it does its own work.
